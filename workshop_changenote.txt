Fixed: the mod did nothing on Stellaris 4.6.

- Stellaris 4.6 silently skipped the mod because of its old title (with "&" and "|"). The game showed no error, the checksum stayed vanilla and you got vanilla points.
- New mod title without those characters: More Picks and Points: Traits, Civics, Ethics [FLEX EDITION]. The files and values are unchanged.
- If it still gives vanilla points after the update, restart the Paradox launcher so it picks up the new name, or unsubscribe and subscribe again.
- Flex Script 2.1.4 uses the new title.
